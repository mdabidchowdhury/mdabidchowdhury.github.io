## SUMMARY

I work on the question of how much control complexity can be offloaded into mechanics — passive mechanical intelligence and minimal-actuation morphologies that substitute mechanism for sensing and computation, validated through coupled analytical modeling, simulation, and full-scale hardware trials. I am also particularly interested in deploying embedded machine learning to improve the real-time environmental interactions of biomimetic aerial and marine robots.
